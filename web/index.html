Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Chengyu Clash collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the app is tracked, recorded, or transmitted to a server.

Local storage only. Your tokens, quiz progress, and preferences are saved on this device using standard browser storage. Deleting the app erases them completely.

Speech. Pronunciation audio is generated by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine and never leaves the device.

Intended audience. The app contains no user-generated content, chat, or external links. It is intended for adult learners: example dialogues include sarcastic and confrontational banter as part of the language instruction.
